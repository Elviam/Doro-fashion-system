# D'oro Fashion System — Risk Analysis

## Context and rating method

This is a practical risk-based testing exercise for the D'oro portfolio. It is based on the current retail workflows, implementation, test plan, manual Sales & Inventory execution, and documented defect. It is not a production-readiness certification, security audit, or quantitative operational-risk assessment.

- **Likelihood:** High when the failure has a common trigger or a known concurrency-sensitive path; Medium when it needs a specific state, timing, or account combination; Low when the preconditions are narrow or strongly constrained.
- **Impact:** High when the failure can affect stock, order state, authorization, or recoverability; Medium when the operation remains recoverable but business records or traceability could be misleading; Low when the effect is limited and does not alter business data.
- **Risk level:** High for any scenario with high impact and at least medium likelihood; Medium for meaningful but contained exposure; Low for limited impact and likelihood.

## Risk response summary

The executed suite gives the strongest evidence for deterministic Sales behavior: stock boundaries, sequential duplicate prevention, state validation, ownership, account-type separation at the API, and transactional rollback. Concurrent payment is tracked separately as R-14 and remains **Not formally executed**; simultaneous cancellation remains residual coverage under R-04. A second residual area is traceability depth: the existing movement model supports product, type, quantity, reason, and timestamp correlation but does not store all audit dimensions as dedicated fields.
